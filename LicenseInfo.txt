-------------------------------Images--------------------------------------

This plugin ships no images and has no icon of its own. The small buttons of its dialogs use
KeePass' own icons, taken at run time from the KeePass installation.

-------------------------------History-------------------------------------

Versions 1.0.x also contained VeraCrypt logos, a picture of the menu, button icons under
non-commercial licenses and a file path regular expression from a Q&A site. Version 2.0.0
removed all of them: the plugin has no icon of its own, its buttons use KeePass' icons,
non-commercial licenses do not fit a GPL program, and the regular expression is no longer used.
